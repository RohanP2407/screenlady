✦ Women-Centric Marathi Publication

About Accolade Screenlady

“Today's woman is no longer confined to household boundaries. She is making her mark in entertainment, politics, industry, business, education, medicine, technology, and beyond.”

Accolade Screenlady is a women-centric Marathi magazine that aims to present the many dimensions of women's personalities and lives to its readers. Going beyond the glamorous public image of actresses, models and artists from the entertainment industry, the magazine seeks to explore their emotions, struggles, experiences, thoughts and the ups and downs of their personal and professional journeys.

Through exclusive interviews with renowned actresses from the film industry, readers get insights into their fashion trends, beauty, acting careers and lesser-known aspects of their lives. However, the scope of Accolade Screenlady extends far beyond the entertainment world. The magazine also gives paramount importance to the inspiring journeys of women leaders in politics, successful doctors, engineers, pilots, administrative officers, women entrepreneurs, business professionals and senior executives in the corporate sector.

✦ Founder, Publisher & Editor

A Vision Inspired by Years of Journalistic Experience

Mr. Prashant Chandrakant Sagvekar • Senior Broadcast Journalist & Conceptualizer

The conceptualizer, publisher and editor of Accolade Screenlady, Mr. Prashant Chandrakant Sagvekar, is a senior journalist with more than 25 years of distinguished experience in the media industry.

His professional experience with reputed media organisations such as All India Radio, Doordarshan Sahyadri, Sahara Samay and Saam Marathi has played an instrumental role in shaping the vision behind this magazine.

Accolade Screenlady was conceptualised with the objective of creating an independent platform where the achievements of women from different walks of life could receive national recognition and their inspiring stories could reach a wider audience.

✦ EDITORIAL VISION & MISSION

Celebrating Women's Achievements, Experiences and Inspiring Journeys

“Accolade Screenlady is dedicated to celebrating the achievements, experiences, and inspiring journeys of women across all walks of life.”

Accolade Screenlady is a women-centric Marathi magazine dedicated to highlighting the achievements, experiences and inspiring journeys of women across various fields, including entertainment, glamour, women empowerment, business, entrepreneurship and social development.

The magazine aims not only to feature well-known personalities but also to bring forward the inspiring stories of ordinary women who have achieved extraordinary success through hard work, determination and perseverance.
